About Me

I am an MSc candidate in Climate Change Engineering at the Pan African University Institute of Water and Energy Sciences (PAUWES), University of Tlemcen, Algeria, and an Assistant Lecturer at the University of Gondar, Ethiopia. My research interests include renewable energy planning, GIS-based spatial analysis, climate adaptation, and sustainable development. I am currently strengthening my skills in Python and GIS to support data-driven climate and energy research.
